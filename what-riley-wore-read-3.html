READ ALOUD · UNIT 1 · WEEK 2

What Riley Wore · Read 3 of 3 · Acting out the story

Friday
BEFORE READING

Set Up the Stage

Seat children around the perimeter of the rug. Explain that the middle is the stage and the edge is the audience. Invite children one at a time. They may pass verbally or with a gesture.

DURING READING

Act Selected Scenes

Pause to let children act selected scenes—or more of the story if your group is ready. Children may play any character, and different children can take turns being Riley.

AFTER READING

Talk About the Experience

Ask what children thought about acting out the story and which part they liked best. Connect to next week’s Dramatic Play dress-up possibilities.

ACTING GUIDE

Tap when you need the next direction
