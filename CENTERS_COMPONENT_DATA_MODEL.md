# Centers Component — Curriculum-Driven Data Model

Centers are generated from:
`Unit → Week → Day → Centers`

Week 1:
- Monday: Art Studio — Drawing; Discovery — Playdough
- Tuesday: Blocks — Exploring Blocks; Library & Listening — Looking at Books
- Wednesday: Dramatic Play — Cooking
- Thursday: Continue center exploration
- Friday: Continue center exploration

The reusable flow is:
1. Centers opening/preview
2. One introduction card per center/activity
3. Centers Ready transition

Each center activity can carry:
- center
- activity
- materials
- childPrompt
- teacherNotes

Visual polish remains intentionally flexible for a later design pass.
