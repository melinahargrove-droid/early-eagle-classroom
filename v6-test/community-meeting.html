Belly Breaths

Get Ready

Sit comfortably. Put one hand on your belly.

Eyes can be open or gently closed.
Belly Breaths

Breathe In

Breathe in slowly. Feel your belly fill up like a balloon.

In… 1 · 2 · 3
Belly Breaths

Breathe Out

Breathe out slowly. Feel your belly get smaller.

Out… 1 · 2 · 3
Belly Breaths

Again

Let’s take a few more slow belly breaths together.

In… 1 · 2 · 3    Out… 1 · 2 · 3
Belly Breaths

All Done

Take one last slow breath. When you’re ready, look back at our group.

Our bodies are ready to learn.
Teacher guidance: Continue Belly Breaths for about 30 seconds. Model a slow inhale and exhale. After the final breath, invite children to slowly return their attention to the group. Source: Focus on Pre-K Week 1 Community Meeting.
