Teacher guidance: Model Belly Breaths for about 30 seconds. Children may keep their eyes open or gently closed. Use a slow three-count inhale and exhale. After the final breath, invite children to return their attention to the group. Source: Focus on Pre-K Week 1 Community Meeting.
