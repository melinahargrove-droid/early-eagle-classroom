EARLY EAGLE ACADEMY · COMMUNITY MEETING
FOCUS ON 3s · UNIT 1 · WEEK 1 · DAY 1

Name Ball

When you have the Name Ball, communicate your name in a way that works for you. The group echoes your name in the same way.

Say it, show it, or point to your name card.
Sit in a circle and introduce a soft ball as the Name Ball. Toss it gently from child to child. When a child has the ball, they communicate their name in any manner, using their name card if helpful, and the group echoes the name in the same manner. Make sure every child has an accessible way to participate.
