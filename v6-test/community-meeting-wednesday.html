Name Ball

UNIT 1 · WEEK 1 · WEDNESDAY
Name Ball

Make a Circle

Sit together in a circle. We are going to play with our Name Ball.

Everyone gets a turn.
Name Ball

Catch the Ball

When the Name Ball comes to you, get ready to share your name.

Catch gently.
Name Ball

Share Your Name

Say or show your name in a way that matches how you feel.

Happy, sleepy, quiet, silly… your way!
Name Ball

We Echo

Everyone repeats your name in the same kind of voice or way.

Listen first. Then echo the name.
Name Ball

Toss to a Friend

Gently toss the Name Ball to another friend. Then we do it again!

Let’s learn everyone’s name.
Teacher guidance: Keep the curriculum concept intact: children toss a soft ball around the circle. The child with the ball communicates their name in a manner that matches their mood, and the group echoes the name in the same manner. Children may say their name, use a name card, or communicate it another way so everyone can participate. Source: Focus on Pre-K Unit 1, Week 1 Community Meeting.
