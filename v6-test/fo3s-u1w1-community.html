Name Ball

Sit together in a circle and introduce a soft ball as the Name Ball.

Gently toss the ball from one child to another.
When a child has the Name Ball, they communicate their name in any manner, including according to their mood.
The group echoes the child’s name in the same manner.
Continue until every child has had an opportunity to participate.
Accessibility: A child may hold up or point to a name card instead of verbally saying their name. Make sure every child has a way to communicate and participate.
