FOCUS ON PRE-K 3s · UNIT 1 · WEEK 1

Adult Storytelling

Week 1 calls for adults to tell stories to model storytelling.

Use the established Storytelling/Acting routine. Tell a short, developmentally appropriate story so children become familiar with listening to a storyteller. The Week 1 Weekly Plan does not prescribe a specific story, so this screen intentionally does not invent one.
