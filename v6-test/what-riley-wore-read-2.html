BEFORE READING

Reading the Pictures

Tell children that this reread is about looking closely at illustrations and using them to describe Riley, feelings, and events.

AFTER READING

Favorite Riley Outfit

Slowly revisit the illustrations and ask which thing Riley wears is their favorite. Connect to drawing themselves in a special outfit and showing a feeling on the face.

TEACHER REMINDER

Let the pictures do the work. Give children time to notice details before you ask for answers.
