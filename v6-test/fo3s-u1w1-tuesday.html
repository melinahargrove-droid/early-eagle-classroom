DAY 2
COMMUNITY MEETING · ONE SCREEN

Name Ball

Repeat Monday’s Name Ball routine: gently pass or toss a soft ball, each child communicates their name in an accessible manner, and the group echoes it.

READ ALOUD

Lola Goes to School · Read 2

Second reading focuses on characters, feelings, and connections. Use the same official adapted Lola book spreads from Monday with the Read 2 curriculum prompts layered at the appropriate pages.

LIBRARY & LISTENING

Looking at Books

  • Connect to Lola reading books in a tent.
  • Children choose a book, begin at the front, and gently turn pages.
  • Model noticing illustrations and wondering about the story.
  • Return each book before choosing another.
BLOCKS

Free Exploration 1

  • Connect to Lola and friends building a block castle.
  • Take blocks from the shelf/bin, build on the floor or carpet, then return blocks to labeled places.
  • Explore sizes and shapes and create a castle or something different.
  • Encourage sharing, collaboration, gentle handling, and respect for structures.
