## Source inconsistencies retained transparently

Mixing Primary Colors initially calls for the yellow+red visual, red and yellow paint for the introduction, and a yellow/red opening invitation. The easel setup instead calls for blue and red paints; the later brush model adds blue to red. All of these directions remain exactly in the teaching notes. The child-facing invitation asks the source's neutral question, “What happens when we mix colors together?” A separate teacher clarification identifies the conflicting pairs and asks the teacher to ensure their chosen materials, visual and demonstration match. No color pair is silently selected or repaired on the classroom's behalf. The source continues to offer two primary colors at a time across the week.

The Week 3 Clubhouse subtitle says “This is a two-part learning experience that continues in Week 2.” That original sentence remains in the purpose notes. A separate clarification documents that the ARC places the continuation in Week 4 and that the lesson introduction says “For the next two weeks.” There is no invented class design, donated-material list, or claim that families have provided items. Source suggestions, including family photos, remain suggestions in the original notes; no child/family photographs or data are collected or bundled.
