Name Ball

Repeat the established Week 1 Name Ball routine.

  • Use a soft ball and gently pass or toss it from child to child.
  • Each child communicates their name in an accessible manner.
  • The group echoes the child’s name in the same manner.
  • Continue until every child has an opportunity to participate.
Accessibility: A child may use or point to a name card instead of speaking.
