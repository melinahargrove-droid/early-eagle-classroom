Let’s try another clue!
EEA-created Week 1 extension: Keep children seated in the circle. Give simple “Find a friend who…” clues and invite children to look, think, then point, name, or otherwise indicate someone who matches. Begin with visible clues such as wearing blue, wearing sneakers, having stripes, or having long sleeves. As children learn more about one another, clues can include preferences such as liking blocks, drawing, or playing outside. More than one child can match; there is no single correct answer. The goal is to notice classmates and discover connections—not to test children. This activity supports the Week 1 classroom-community goals but is not a Focus on Pre-K prescribed activity.
