READ ALOUD

Naming Ceremony · Read 1 of 2

  • Reinforce Read Aloud expectations with the meeting agreements visual: look at the pictures, listen to the words, and talk together after the story.
  • Introduce Naming Ceremony by Seina Wedlick, illustrated by Jenin Mohammed. Introduce Amira and explain that her family is welcoming a new baby and will have a special naming ceremony.
  • Page 1: Amira is excited—happy and unable to wait. Connect to excitement about the first week of school; invite a thumbs up.
  • Page 8: Define tradition as something a family or community does again and again because it is special.
  • Page 11: Define thankful as feeling happy about having or doing something. Connect to being thankful for classroom play; invite a thumbs up.
  • Page 21: Notice Amira looks nervous. Connect her tight hands/worried face to children’s experiences of feeling nervous; invite a thumbs up.
  • Page 25: Define celebrate as doing something special for a person or important day. Ask how children’s families like to celebrate.
  • Page 26: Explain that the baby is named Habiba and that beloved means very, very loved and special.
  • After reading, page 27: Ask what happened at the naming ceremony for Amira’s baby sister.
  • Closing: Connect to Centers: children can pretend to have a celebration and prepare food in Dramatic Play, like Amira’s family.
