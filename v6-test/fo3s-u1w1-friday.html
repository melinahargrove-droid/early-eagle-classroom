Naming Ceremony · Read 2 of 2

  • Reintroduce the book and Amira and her baby sister. Invite thumbs up from children who know a baby.
  • This read is not cover-to-cover. Begin on page 9 and revisit selected characters and events.
  • Pages 9–10, 13–14: briefly revisit Grandma Nana, cousins, Miki and other characters; invite children to chime in with names.
  • Page 11: look closely at the illustration. Ask what is happening; connect to the family preparing food for the celebration.
  • Page 15: revisit Uncle Ali playing his drum. Draw attention to the large print and read the sound words together while tracking the print.
  • Pages 16–26: flip through, revisiting characters and events and inviting children to contribute what they remember.
  • Discussion, page 27: notice family and friends bringing gifts. Ask children to think of a time they did something thoughtful for someone else.
  • Closing: connect the special names in the story to children’s own names and suggest writing their name on artwork or asking a teacher to write it.
