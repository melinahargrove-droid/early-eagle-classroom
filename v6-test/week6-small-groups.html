EARLY EAGLE ACADEMY · WEEK 6

Literacy Small Groups

ADULT-LED WRITING

Writing Basics • Sketch a Story

Children think of a true story about themselves and sketch one part of it with pencil, then add color and details. Emphasize that writers can communicate through images.

ADULT SUPPORT

Skills Reinforcement

Select foundational literacy skills to revisit based on children’s needs, aligned with Heggerty Week 3.

KNOWLEDGE BUILDING

My Day with Gong Gong • Sequencing

Use the sequencing cards to put important events in order from first to last: Gong Gong arrives; neighborhood walk; May cries after the bird incident; Gong Gong gives the gift; May and Gong Gong hug and say “I love you.”

Ask: What do you think is the most important event in the story?

MINIMAL SUPPORT

Fine Motor Development

Choose activities based on observation and individual goals to build fine-motor skills and stamina.
