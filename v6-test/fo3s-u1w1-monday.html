DAY 1 MASTER
1

Community Meeting

Name Ball

  • Sit in a circle and introduce a soft ball as the Name Ball.
  • Toss it gently from child to child.
  • The child communicates their name in any manner; the group echoes it in the same manner.
  • A child may use or point to a name card instead of speaking.
Fo3s U1W1 Community Meeting
2

Read Aloud

Lola Goes to School · Read 1 of 2

  • Introduce Unit 1: Family and Friends and the Read Aloud routine.
  • Introduce title, author Anna McQuinn, and illustrator Rosalind Beardshaw.
  • Read the full text. Curriculum stops: page 3 visit; page 10 getting ready for school; page 11 welcome.
  • After reading ask what Lola does at school, then bridge to Centers.
Fo3s U1W1 Lola Goes to School · Read 1
3

Writing & Drawing

Crayon and Marker Exploration

  • Introduce the Writing and Drawing Center.
  • Children choose markers or crayons and create on paper.
  • Model removing and replacing marker caps until they click.
  • Teach that markers and crayons are for paper and model clean-up.
Fo3s U1W1 Crayon and Marker Exploration
4

Science & Engineering

Exploring Playdough

  • Introduce Science and Engineering.
  • Use touch, smell, and sight to explore playdough.
  • Model rolling, pinching, pressing, and other ways hands can change its shape.
  • Ask what children notice about how it looks, smells, and feels.
Fo3s U1W1 Exploring Playdough
5

Storytelling & Acting

Adult Storytelling

  • Week 1 calls for adults to tell stories to model storytelling.
  • Use the established classroom Storytelling/Acting routine as children become familiar with listening to stories.
