Familiar Books From Home

U1 · W1 · WEDNESDAY
📚

Share a Familiar Favorite

  • Select one or two favorite books contributed by families, books connected to children’s interests, or a familiar classroom book if none were sent.
  • Introduce whose favorite book it is, the title, author, and illustrator. Invite the child to tell the group a little about the book.
  • Activate background knowledge and give children something to listen/read to find out.
  • Read the full text fluently. Choose 2–3 appropriate pauses to define vocabulary, ask what is happening, or use illustrations to think about a character’s feelings.
  • After reading, ask 1–2 questions such as why the child likes the book, why it is important to the child/family, or what their favorite part is.
  • Thank the child/family and place the book in Library & Listening for children to revisit.
Teacher note: There is intentionally no Adapted/Full Text toggle because the curriculum does not prescribe one standardized book for this experience.
