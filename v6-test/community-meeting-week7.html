Community Meeting

This curriculum provides a Week 7 bank rather than assigning one item to each weekday. Choose from the options below as appropriate.

Mindful Practice · Butterfly Pose

Sit tall, bend the legs, put the soles of the feet together, and gently flap the legs like butterfly wings. Slowly straighten the legs and relax. Children may also flap elbows with palms together or gently move open arms up and down.

Mindful Practice · Elephant Breath

Stand with feet apart and hands linked, arms dangling like a trunk. Breathe in through the nose while raising the arms overhead and leaning back. Breathe out through the mouth while swinging the arms down. Repeat three times, then return to normal breathing.

Conversation Prompts

Alma: Alma’s father tells her stories about her names. What kind of stories do you like to hear about?

Hair Love: Zuri’s father takes care of her hair as a way of showing kindness. How can you show kindness to others?

Games

Hot Hot Hands: Rub hands together, stop, place the warmed hands on ears, then repeat with body parts children suggest.

Body Shakes: Children suggest a body part to shake; everyone follows the motion together, then stops on the teacher’s gentle signal.

Problem Stories

Use the Problem Stories block characters to act out a short scenario based on something children are struggling with, such as sharing.

Teacher Reflection

Notice children’s affect during and after mindful exercises, their responses to Problem Stories, and how they share ideas during group conversations.
