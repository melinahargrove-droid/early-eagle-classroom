Literacy Small Groups

Writing · Adult-Led Instruction

Objective: Tell a story about an experience in my life.

Connect to My Day With Gong Gong. Take a virtual field trip to the Boston Children’s Museum using the curriculum slides, pretending to enter and explore three rooms. At each stop, children interact with the listed materials. On the final slide, notice the exit sign and pretend to leave.

Closing: Orally retell the virtual field trip together. The curriculum notes that next week children will write about this shared experience.

Knowledge Building · Hair Love Sequencing

Introduce after reading Hair Love on Day 3. Identify what is happening on each sequencing card, then work together to put the events in order from first to last. Finish by asking which event children think is most important.
