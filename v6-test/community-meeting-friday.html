EEA-created Week 1 extension: Use this as a short, warm reflection on the children's first week together. Invite children to notice their classmates and recall something they learned about the classroom or a friend. Reinforce that every child is a valued member of the group and that a classroom community learns, plays, and cares for the space together. Keep sharing optional and accept pointing, gestures, brief words, or other ways children communicate. This activity supports the Week 1 classroom-community goals but is not a Focus on Pre-K prescribed activity.
