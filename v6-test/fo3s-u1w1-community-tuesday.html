NAME BALL

Name Ball

Repeat the Name Ball routine so children continue learning and practicing one another’s names.

Sit together in a circle and use a soft ball as the Name Ball.
Gently toss or pass the ball from child to child.
The child communicates their name in any manner; the group echoes it in the same manner.
Continue until every child has an opportunity to participate.
Accessibility: A child may use or point to a name card instead of speaking.
