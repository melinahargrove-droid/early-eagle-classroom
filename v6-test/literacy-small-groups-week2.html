TODAY'S GAME

Clothing Memory

Can you find two clothes that match?
  1. 1Turn over two cards.
  2. 2Look carefully. Do they match?
  3. 3Take turns until time is up.
MATERIALS & PREPARATION

Clothing visual cards

Prepare 2 sets of clothing visual cards. Look at the cards together first and identify the pieces of clothing.

HOW TO PLAY

Model one turn first

Place the cards face down in rows. Children take turns flipping two cards to look for a match. Put matches together in a community pile and continue as time permits.

END WITH

Quick conversation

Ask: “What is your favorite piece of clothing? How do you feel wearing it?”

IF NEEDED

Keep the cards face up and have children match the pairs.

Fine Motor Development: choose activities based on observation and children’s individual fine-motor goals.
