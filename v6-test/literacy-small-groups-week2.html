UNIT 1 · WEEK 2 · LITERACY SMALL GROUPS

Clothing Memory

Knowledge Building · adult check-in / guidance
Materials & Preparation

Clothing visual cards

Prepare 2 sets of clothing visual cards.

Introduce the game

Tell children that the cards show different pieces of clothing. First, look together and identify the clothing on the cards.

Place all cards face down in rows. Children take turns flipping two cards to look for a match. Put matches together in a community pile and continue as time permits.

Teacher Guidance

Model first

Show children how to take a turn, answer questions, and begin the game.

At the end ask: “What is your favorite piece of clothing? How do you feel wearing it?”

Alternative: keep the cards face up and have children match the pairs.
