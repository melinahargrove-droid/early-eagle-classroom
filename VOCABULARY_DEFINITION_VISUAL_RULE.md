# Daily Lessons Vocabulary Definition Visual Rule

## Permanent rule
Every vocabulary/definition slide must include a clear, child-friendly visual example of the word or concept.

### Requirements
- The visual must directly demonstrate the meaning; it may not be merely decorative.
- Whenever possible, show the word in context or in action.
- The visual must be large and clear enough to understand from the classroom SmartBoard.
- Use a preschool-appropriate illustration or relevant curriculum/book visual when appropriate.
- Keep the written definition short, concrete, and child-friendly.
- Vocabulary/definition content stays on its own slide and must never reduce the size of a Read-the-Story book spread.
- Apply this rule automatically to all future Read Aloud vocabulary slides and other Daily Lessons definition slides.

### Standard vocabulary slide pattern
**Word → simple definition → meaningful visual example**

Example: for “jumping pants,” show a child actively jumping/playing while wearing comfortable pants rather than an isolated picture of pants.
