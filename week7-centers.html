Week 7 Centers Guide

Art Easel · Painting Inspired by Goodbye Summer, Hello Autumn

Children continue mixing colors and use illustrations, the outdoors, or other ideas as inspiration for paintings. Collaboration is encouraged.

Art Studio · Representing Hair

Children use Beautiful Stuff to represent their own hair, arranging materials on paper or trays and documenting creations with photos.

Blocks · Building Furniture

Use unit blocks and Beautiful Stuff to make furniture. Discuss what different furniture is for and use familiar book illustrations as inspiration.

Discovery · Mixing Salt Dough

Continue mixing salt dough, this week adding optional color and/or scent. Children notice how new ingredients change the mixture.

Dramatic Play · Caring for Hair

Children pretend to care for dolls’ hair using hair-care tools and props. If classmates participate, permission must be asked before touching anyone’s hair.

Library & Listening · Retelling Stories

Add Alma and How She Got Her Name, Hair Love, and character puppets. Encourage children to refer to the books while retelling and extending stories.

Science & Engineering · Observing Ourselves

Children use mirrors, magnifiers, eye droppers, and their senses to observe hair, skin, and other features, then record observations through drawing.

Writing & Drawing · Family Pictures

Children draw the people and relationships they identify as family. Adults affirm the range of family structures and write down names or words children use.
