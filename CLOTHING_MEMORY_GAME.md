# Clothing Memory Game
Reusable interactive pop-out attached to Week 2 Literacy Small Group: Clothing Memory.
- Default: 20 cards / 10 matching pairs.
- Teacher may switch to 10 cards / 5 pairs.
- Cards flip on tap; matches remain face up; nonmatches flip back.
- Completion screen: Play Again or Back to Lesson.
- Closing returns to the exact Small Group lesson card.
- Component is reusable for future matching-game datasets.
