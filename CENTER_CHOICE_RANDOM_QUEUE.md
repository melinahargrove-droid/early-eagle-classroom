# Center Choice Random Turn Queue

Center Choice now includes a lightweight randomized turn-order tool.

Flow:
1. Tap **Choose Who’s Next**.
2. The app uses the same saved class roster and excludes students marked absent.
3. It shuffles the present students once.
4. One child’s name appears while all center cards remain visible.
5. After the child chooses a center, tap **Next Friend**.
6. Every child appears exactly once.
7. After the final child, the bar displays **Everyone has chosen!**
8. **Shuffle Again** creates a fresh order.

This is separate from the full Random Student feature. It is a randomized queue designed specifically for Center Choice.
