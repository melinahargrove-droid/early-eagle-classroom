# v5.9.26 — Daily Star reveal privacy

- The Star of the Day name stays hidden on the Home dashboard until the reveal is tapped.
- Before the reveal, the Home panel says “Tap to Reveal!” instead of showing the child’s name.
- Revealing the star updates the Home panel immediately.
- The reveal is remembered for that child on that calendar day, including after closing/reopening the pop-out or refreshing the app.
- A new day or a different Star of the Day automatically returns to the hidden state.
