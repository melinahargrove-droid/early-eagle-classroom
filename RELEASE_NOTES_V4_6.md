# Early Eagle Academy Companion v4.6

## What’s Next panel lock
- Keeps the approved Home Dashboard appearance unchanged.
- What’s Next remains automatically driven by Today’s Schedule.
- Activity name, time, and message update immediately as schedule progress changes.
- The panel is presentation-only: it cannot be clicked or tapped to launch tools.
- When all activities are complete, it displays “All Done!” and the end-of-day message.
