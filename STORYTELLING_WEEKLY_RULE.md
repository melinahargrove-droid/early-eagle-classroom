# Storytelling Weekly Rule

- Monday-Thursday: teacher tells one short original story connected to the current week's curriculum.
- Each teacher story has a one-page printable PDF linked from the Storytelling screen.
- Child-facing screen shows the story title and one simple scene illustration; story text is teacher-only.
- Friday: one child tells an original story.
- Friday offers 2-3 visual idea prompts. The picture is an idea starter, not a prescribed story.
- Teacher-only scaffolds: Who is in your story? Where are they? What happens? What happens at the end?
